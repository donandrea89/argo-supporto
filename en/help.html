Argo help

From first launch to your first film in six steps. You need your NAS switched on, the Argo app and your home Wi-Fi. Questions: info@donandreaalfieri.com.

Getting started

  1. Accept the terms. On first launch Argo shows the Terms of use and the Privacy notice (in Italian; the Italian text prevails). Nothing opens until you accept.
  2. Argo finds your NAS. Join the same Wi-Fi as the NAS; Argo looks for it by itself.
  3. Install Argo on the NAS. On Synology, QNAP and TrueNAS, type the NAS administrator name and password: they go only to your NAS, on your home network, and are never stored. On other brands the app prepares a file to paste into the NAS Docker panel (see the Install page, in Italian).
  4. Create your profile. You become the Argo administrator on that NAS.
  5. Pick your film folders. Argo only reads them: it never moves, renames or deletes a file.
  6. Profiles and away from home (optional). Settings → Profiles for each person at home; Settings → Away from home for a direct connection through your router or through a VPS of your own.

If something goes wrong

The app cannot find the NAS
  • Make sure the device is on your home Wi-Fi, not mobile data or a guest network.
  • Check in the NAS Docker panel that the «argo» project is running.
  • Some routers isolate Wi-Fi clients from wired devices: turn that off, or type the NAS address by hand.
One-tap install stops
  • Synology needs Container Manager and the «docker» shared folder (open Container Manager once to create it).
  • QNAP needs Container Station 3, opened at least once.
  • TrueNAS SCALE 25.04 or later needs Apps enabled: Apps → Configuration → Choose Pool.
  • If it still stops, follow the manual steps on the Install page.
The catalogue is empty or films are missing

Open Diagnostics: an unreadable folder has been moved, renamed, or its disk is not mounted. After adding films, press «Update catalogue» in Settings.

Playback stutters or starts slowly

The NAS may have to convert the film for your device, and 4K takes a lot of power. Pick a lower quality in Settings. Away from home, speed also depends on your home upload bandwidth.

Away from home does not work

For the direct connection your router must forward UDP port 51821 to the NAS, and your public home name (the NAS brand’s DDNS) must be up to date. If your provider gives no public address (CGNAT), use the VPS option.

Using Diagnostics

In the app: Settings → Diagnostics, with an administrator profile. From a browser at home: open http://your-nas-address:8787, sign in, then «La tua esperienza» → Diagnostica. Press «Copy report» and paste it into your email: it contains no passwords, keys or file names.

Frequently asked questions

Does Argo send my data anywhere?

No. The app talks only to your NAS: no cloud account and no server of Argo’s author in between, not even away from home.

Can I watch films without a NAS?

No. Argo connects your device to your own server and provides no content. Use only content you have the right to play.

Is Argo available in English?

The apps are in Italian during the beta. English is planned before version 1.0.
