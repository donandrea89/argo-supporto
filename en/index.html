Beta 0.6 on TestFlight. Argo for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV is under review by Apple. Testing on NAS models from different brands is under way. The app is in Italian for now.

Every story,
a journey.

Films and TV series from your own NAS, with a private cinema on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Your catalogue

Films, series, seasons, episodes, sagas and lists, straight from the folders on your NAS.

Any NAS

One-tap install on Synology, QNAP and TrueNAS; a ready-made file for UGREEN, Asustor, TerraMaster, Unraid and Linux.

Private by design

The app talks only to your NAS. No cloud account, and nobody else sees what you watch, even away from home.

Free, and yours

Argo will be free. It needs a compatible server and content you are allowed to play. It includes no films, subscriptions or cloud catalogue.
